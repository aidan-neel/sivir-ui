## Sidebar

`Sidebar` is a namespaced compound component for the app shell. Compose it as `Sidebar.Root` containing one `Sidebar.Panel` and one `Sidebar.Inset`. Root sets no height. Give it one (`h-dvh` for a full page, or a fixed height inside a frame) or the panel and inset have nothing to fill. Put page content in `Sidebar.Inset`, which renders `<main>`. Do not wrap it in another `<main>`.

Collapse state lives on Root. `bind:open` controls the desktop panel and defaults to `true`. `bind:openMobile` controls the drawer and defaults to `false`. `collapsible` picks the collapsed form: `offcanvas` (the default) slides the panel fully out, `icon` keeps a narrow rail of icons, and `none` never collapses. `variant="inset"` draws the main area as a rounded card inset from the panel. `side="right"` goes on `Sidebar.Panel`, not on Root, and the panel moves to the end of the row whichever order it appears in. `Sidebar.Trigger` toggles whichever form is active. Below 768px the panel always renders as a modal drawer portaled to `<body>`, and following a link closes it.

Build the panel from `Sidebar.Header`, `Sidebar.Content` (the scrolling region) and `Sidebar.Footer`. Inside Content, each `Sidebar.Group` holds an optional `Sidebar.GroupLabel` and a `Sidebar.GroupContent` that wraps a `Sidebar.Menu` of `Sidebar.Item` rows. Each Item holds one `Sidebar.ItemButton` (it becomes a link when given `href`, and `active` marks the current page), plus an optional `Sidebar.ItemBadge` and `Sidebar.ItemAction`. Keep the badge and action as direct children of the Item, after the ItemButton. The row's padding and the hover reveal depend on that sibling order. `Sidebar.ItemAction` requires `aria-label`. To nest items, put a `Sidebar.Menu` inside a `Sidebar.Item`, after its ItemButton, and it indents automatically.

For the icon rail, wrap every row label in `Sidebar.ItemLabel` and put the icon directly inside the ItemButton. The rail hides ItemLabel visually but keeps it in the accessibility tree. Pass `tooltip` on ItemButton to show the label on hover in the rail. Group labels, badges, actions, and nested menus hide in the rail, and collapsible groups stay open there. `<Sidebar.Group collapsible>` turns its label into a toggle. `open` and `onOpenChange` are only valid when `collapsible` is set.

Row hover and focus come from the shared traveling highlight, not from a background on the row. `Sidebar.Header`, `Sidebar.Content` and `Sidebar.Footer` each own one highlight surface, and every `Sidebar.ItemButton` inside them joins it. Keep menus inside one of those three parts: a `Sidebar.Menu` placed directly in the Panel gets no hover feedback. Do not add `hover:bg-*` classes to ItemButton, since they would stack on the highlight. `active` keeps its own `bg-secondary`, and the highlight fades out when the pointer leaves instead of resting on the active row. Themes that set `chrome.travelingHighlight: false` fall back to a per-row background automatically.
